# Phase 02: Microservices Fundamentals

Once a monolith reaches its limits, organizations often look toward Microservices. However, Microservices are not just a technical architecture; they represent a fundamental shift in how teams are organized and how software is designed. 

In this phase, we explore the core tenets of microservices and the methodology used to design them properly: Domain-Driven Design (DDD).

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this phase, you will understand:
- The defining characteristics of a microservice.
- How to divide a large system into smaller services using Bounded Contexts (DDD).
- The difference between modern Microservices and legacy Service-Oriented Architecture (SOA).

## Files in this Phase

1. `01-What-are-Microservices.md`: Core principles (independent deployability, decentralized data).
2. `02-Domain-Driven-Design.md`: How to find the boundaries of a microservice to prevent high coupling.
3. `03-Microservices-vs-SOA.md`: Understanding the historical context and why the Enterprise Service Bus (ESB) failed.
