# Phase 7: Series, Coding-Decoding, and Blood Relations

## What You'll Learn

This phase opens the Logical Reasoning track, following the 6-phase Quantitative Aptitude block. It's a different kind of thinking: instead of applying formulas, you're spotting patterns and following chains of clues systematically. Start with number and letter series — recognizing arithmetic, geometric, alternating, and second-order patterns, plus converting letters to alphabet positions to solve letter series and odd-one-out questions. Move into coding-decoding — letter-shift ciphers, number-substitution codes, and the "common word / common code" trick for decoding coded sentences. Finish with blood relations and direction sense — drawing quick family-tree diagrams to untangle multi-generation relationship puzzles, and tracking net displacement (with Pythagoras' theorem) through a sequence of directional turns.

## Learning Objectives

- Spot number and letter series patterns quickly by writing out the difference row and testing arithmetic, geometric, alternating, and second-order rules in order
- Decode letter-shift and number-substitution codes, and extract a specific word's code from a pair of coded sentences using the common-word/common-code method
- Solve blood-relation and direction-sense puzzles by diagramming — a family tree for relations, and running North-South/East-West totals for direction sense
