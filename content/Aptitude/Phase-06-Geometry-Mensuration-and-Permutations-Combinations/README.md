# Phase 6: Geometry, Mensuration, and Permutations & Combinations

## What You'll Learn

Extend mensuration beyond the basics covered in Phase 5 into the shapes that actually challenge test-takers: right triangles via Pythagoras' theorem, triangles where only the three sides are known via Heron's formula, circular sectors, and the two curved 3D solids — cones and spheres — with both volume and surface area. Then pivot into counting: permutations versus combinations (the single question of whether order matters), factorial-cancellation shortcuts, circular permutations, and permutations with repeated items. Finish with probability — the favorable/total ratio, the addition and multiplication rules, card/dice/coin problems, and the complementary-probability trick that turns "at least one" problems from multi-step sums into a single subtraction. This phase also marks the end of the 6-phase Quantitative Aptitude block; Phase 7 begins the Logical Reasoning track.

## Learning Objectives

- Apply core geometry and mensuration formulas fast — Pythagoras' theorem, Heron's formula, sector area/arc length, and cone/sphere volume and surface area
- Distinguish permutation problems from combination problems on sight (does order matter?), and apply circular-permutation and repeated-item shortcuts correctly
- Apply the complement trick (1 − P(not A)) to simplify "at least one" probability problems, and correctly distinguish dependent from independent events
