# Phase 3: Ratio, Proportion, and Partnership

## What You'll Learn

Master ratio manipulation as a fast, algebra-free tool: simplifying and compounding ratios, splitting a total quantity by a given ratio, and correctly telling direct proportion apart from inverse proportion. Then apply that same ratio machinery to partnership problems, where profit has to be split fairly by capital, and — when investment periods differ — by capital weighted by time.

## Learning Objectives

- Simplify and compound ratios quickly, and divide a total quantity in a given ratio without setting up algebra
- Distinguish direct proportion from inverse proportion and apply the correct cross-multiplication or constant-product rule
- Solve partnership profit-sharing problems, including capital × time weighting for partners who join late or leave early, and fixed working-partner fees
