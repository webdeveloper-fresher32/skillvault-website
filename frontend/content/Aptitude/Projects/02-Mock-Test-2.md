## Section A: Quantitative Aptitude (Q1-15)

1. Find the unit digit of 3⁹⁷.
   A) 1  B) 3  C) 7  D) 9

2. The HCF of two numbers is 8 and their LCM is 144. If one of the numbers is 16, find the other number.
   A) 56  B) 64  C) 72  D) 80

3. Simplify: 15 − 6 ÷ 3 + 4 × 2
   A) 17  B) 19  C) 21  D) 23

4. Find the value of 30% of 300 + 20% of 150.
   A) 100  B) 110  C) 120  D) 130

5. A shopkeeper marks an item 25% above its cost price, then offers a 12% discount on the marked price. Find his overall profit percent.
   A) 8%  B) 10%  C) 12%  D) 15%

6. In what ratio must rice worth ₹40/kg be mixed with rice worth ₹55/kg so that the mixture is worth ₹50/kg?
   A) 1:2  B) 2:1  C) 1:1  D) 3:2

7. If A:B = 4:7 and B:C = 3:5, find A:B:C.
   A) 4:7:5  B) 12:21:35  C) 12:21:15  D) 4:21:35

8. A invests ₹7000 for 8 months and B invests ₹6000 for 10 months in a business. Find the ratio of their profit shares.
   A) 14:15  B) 15:14  C) 7:6  D) 6:7

9. A car travels at 80 km/h for 3.5 hours. Find the distance covered.
   A) 260 km  B) 270 km  C) 280 km  D) 300 km

10. A train 180 m long crosses a pole in 12 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
    A) 48 km/h  B) 50 km/h  C) 54 km/h  D) 60 km/h

11. A can complete a piece of work in 12 days and B can complete it in 20 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
    A) 6 days  B) 7 days  C) 7.5 days  D) 8 days

12. Find the compound interest on ₹2000 for 2 years at 5% per annum, compounded annually.
    A) ₹195  B) ₹200  C) ₹205  D) ₹210

13. A container has 100 liters of pure milk. 20 liters are removed and replaced with water; this operation is repeated once more (a total of two times). How much pure milk remains?
    A) 56 L  B) 60 L  C) 64 L  D) 68 L

14. A right-angled triangle has legs of 9 cm and 12 cm. Find its hypotenuse.
    A) 13 cm  B) 14 cm  C) 15 cm  D) 16 cm

15. A bag contains 6 red and 4 blue balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that both are blue.
    A) 2/15  B) 4/15  C) 1/5  D) 3/10

## Section B: Logical Reasoning (Q16-25)

16. Find the missing number in the series: 4, 12, 10, 30, 28, 84, ?

    A) T  B) U  C) V  D) W

    A) YQTNF  B) XPSME  C) YRTNF  D) YQSNE

19. In a certain code, MOBILE is written as 493785 and TABLET is written as 213852. Using the same code, what is the code for TABLE?
    A) 21385  B) 23185  C) 21835  D) 23815

20. A is the father of B. B is the mother of C. D is the brother of C. How is A related to D?
    A) Grandfather  B) Father  C) Uncle  D) Brother

21. Pointing to a photograph, a man says, "She is the only daughter of my father's only sister." How is the woman related to the man?
    A) Sister  B) Niece  C) Cousin  D) Daughter

22. A woman walks 7 km South, then turns left and walks 24 km. How far is she from her starting point?
    A) 17 km  B) 25 km  C) 31 km  D) 24 km

## Answer Key

1. **B) 3** — Unit digit of powers of 3 cycles as 3, 9, 7, 1 (period 4). 97 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 1, so the answer is the 1st term of the cycle: 3.
2. **C) 72** — HCF × LCM = product of the two numbers, so the other number = (8 × 144) ÷ 16 = 72. (Check: HCF(16, 72) = 8, LCM(16, 72) = 144. ✓)
3. **C) 21** — Division and multiplication left to right first: 6 ÷ 3 = 2; 4 × 2 = 8; then 15 − 2 + 8 = 21.
4. **C) 120** — 30% of 300 = 90; 20% of 150 = 30; total = 120.
5. **B) 10%** — CP = 100; MP = 125 (25% above CP); SP = 125 × 0.88 = 110; profit = 10, so profit% = 10%.
6. **A) 1:2** — Alligation: Cheaper : Dearer = (55−50) : (50−40) = 5 : 10 = 1:2.
7. **B) 12:21:35** — A:B = 4:7 = 12:21; B:C = 3:5 = 21:35; combining on the common B value (21) gives A:B:C = 12:21:35.
8. **A) 14:15** — Profit ratio = (investment × time): A = 7000×8 = 56000; B = 6000×10 = 60000; ratio = 56000:60000 = 14:15.
9. **C) 280 km** — Distance = speed × time = 80 × 3.5 = 280 km.
10. **C) 54 km/h** — Speed = 180 m ÷ 12 s = 15 m/s; converting, 15 × 18/5 = 54 km/h.
11. **C) 7.5 days** — Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/20 = 5/60 + 3/60 = 8/60 = 2/15, so together they take 15/2 = 7.5 days.
12. **C) ₹205** — CI = P(1+R/100)ᵀ − P = 2000×(1.05)² − 2000 = 2205 − 2000 = 205.
13. **C) 64 L** — Each removal-and-replace step multiplies remaining milk by (1 − 20/100) = 0.8. After 2 steps: 100 × 0.8² = 100 × 0.64 = 64 L.
14. **C) 15 cm** — 9, 12, 15 is a multiple of the 3-4-5 Pythagorean triple: 9² + 12² = 81 + 144 = 225 = 15².
15. **A) 2/15** — P(both blue) = C(4,2)/C(10,2) = 6/45 = 2/15.
16. **B) 82** — The operations alternate ×3, −2: 4×3=12, 12−2=10, 10×3=30, 30−2=28, 28×3=84, 84−2=82.
17. **B) U** — Alphabet positions C(3), F(6), J(10), O(15) have differences 3, 4, 5; the differences themselves increase by 1, so the next difference is 6, giving position 15+6=21 = U.
18. **A) YQTNF** — Confirm the +2 shift from HELLO→JGNNQ, then apply it to WORLD: W→Y, O→Q, R→T, L→N, D→F.
19. **A) 21385** — The shared letters B, L, E have consistent digits in both given codes (B=3, L=8, E=5), and T=2, A=1 come from TABLET. So TABLE = T(2) A(1) B(3) L(8) E(5) = 21385.
20. **A) Grandfather** — A is B's father; B is the mother of both C and D (D is C's brother), so A is D's (maternal) grandfather.
21. **C) Cousin** — "My father's only sister" is the man's paternal aunt; her only daughter is, by definition, the man's cousin.
22. **B) 25 km** — South 7 km, then facing South a left turn means now facing East; walking 24 km East. Net displacement is 7 km and 24 km along perpendicular axes, so distance = √(7²+24²) = √(49+576) = √625 = 25 km (the 7-24-25 triple).
23. **A) U, P, R, S, Q, T** — Exactly three people to the right of R means R is seat 3 (of 6); S immediately right of R gives seat 4; U at the left end gives seat 1; T at the right end gives seat 6; Q immediately left of T gives seat 5; the only remaining seat (2) goes to P.
24. **B) C** — Facing the centre, B (immediate right of A) and D (immediate left of A) occupy the two seats adjacent to A, leaving only the far seat — directly opposite A — for C.
25. **B) Tuesday** — Coding = Monday and Deployment = Thursday are fixed; Testing immediately before Deployment gives Wednesday; the only day left, Tuesday, goes to Design.
26. **B) Definitely** — The word ends in "-itely," not "-ately" or "-itly."
27. **A) walk** — The plural subject "They" takes the base verb form "walk" in the present simple tense.
28. **B) Easy** — "Difficult" means hard to do; its opposite is "Easy." ("Hard," "Tough," and "Complex" are synonyms of "Difficult," not antonyms.)
29. **C) She doesn't know the answer.** — Third-person singular subject "She" needs the auxiliary "doesn't," followed by the base form of the verb "know."
30. **C) Huge** — "Big" and "Huge" both describe large size; the other options mean small.
