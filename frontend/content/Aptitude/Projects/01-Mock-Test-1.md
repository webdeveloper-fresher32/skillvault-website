## Section A: Quantitative Aptitude (Q1-15)

1. Find the unit digit of 7¹²³.
   A) 1  B) 3  C) 7  D) 9

2. The HCF of two numbers is 12 and their LCM is 240. If one of the numbers is 48, find the other number.
   A) 40  B) 50  C) 60  D) 72

3. Simplify: 12 + 8 ÷ 4 × 2 − 3
   A) 9  B) 11  C) 13  D) 15

4. Find the value of 40% of 250 + 25% of 160.
   A) 120  B) 130  C) 140  D) 150

5. A shopkeeper marks an item 20% above its cost price, then offers a 10% discount on the marked price. Find his overall profit percent.
   A) 5%  B) 8%  C) 10%  D) 12%

6. In what ratio must tea worth ₹60/kg be mixed with tea worth ₹75/kg so that the mixture is worth ₹65/kg?
   A) 1:2  B) 2:1  C) 1:1  D) 3:2

7. If A:B = 3:5 and B:C = 2:3, find A:B:C.
   A) 3:5:8  B) 6:10:15  C) 3:2:5  D) 6:10:9

8. A invests ₹5000 for 12 months and B invests ₹6000 for 8 months in a business. Find the ratio of their profit shares.
   A) 5:4  B) 3:2  C) 5:6  D) 4:5

9. A car travels at 60 km/h for 2.5 hours. Find the distance covered.
   A) 120 km  B) 135 km  C) 150 km  D) 175 km

10. A train 150 m long crosses a pole in 15 seconds. Find its speed in km/h.
    A) 27 km/h  B) 30 km/h  C) 36 km/h  D) 45 km/h

11. A can complete a piece of work in 10 days and B can complete it in 15 days. Working together, in how many days will they finish it?
    A) 5 days  B) 6 days  C) 7.5 days  D) 12 days

12. Find the compound interest on ₹1000 for 2 years at 10% per annum, compounded annually.
    A) ₹190  B) ₹200  C) ₹210  D) ₹220

13. A container has 50 liters of pure milk. 10 liters are removed and replaced with water; this operation is repeated once more (a total of two times). How much pure milk remains?
    A) 25 L  B) 30 L  C) 32 L  D) 36 L

14. A right-angled triangle has legs of 8 cm and 15 cm. Find its hypotenuse.
    A) 16 cm  B) 17 cm  C) 18 cm  D) 20 cm

15. A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. Two balls are drawn at random without replacement. Find the probability that both are red.
    A) 5/28  B) 5/14  C) 3/14  D) 15/28

## Section C: Verbal Ability (Q26-30)

26. Choose the correctly spelled word.

27. Fill in the blank: "She ____ to the market every day."

## Answer Key

1. **B) 3** — The unit digit of powers of 7 cycles as 7, 9, 3, 1 (period 4). 123 ÷ 4 leaves remainder 3, so the answer is the 3rd term of the cycle: 3.
2. **C) 60** — HCF × LCM = product of the two numbers, so the other number = (12 × 240) ÷ 48 = 60.
3. **C) 13** — Division and multiplication left to right first: 8 ÷ 4 × 2 = 4; then 12 + 4 − 3 = 13.
4. **C) 140** — 40% of 250 = 100; 25% of 160 = 40; total = 140.
5. **B) 8%** — CP = 100; MP = 120 (20% above CP); SP = 120 × 0.9 = 108; profit = 8, so profit% = 8%.
6. **B) 2:1** — Alligation: Cheaper : Dearer = (75−65) : (65−60) = 10 : 5 = 2:1.
7. **B) 6:10:15** — A:B = 3:5 = 6:10; B:C = 2:3 = 10:15; combining on the common B value (10) gives A:B:C = 6:10:15.
8. **A) 5:4** — Profit ratio = (investment × time): A = 5000×12 = 60000; B = 6000×8 = 48000; ratio = 60000:48000 = 5:4.
9. **C) 150 km** — Distance = speed × time = 60 × 2.5 = 150 km.
10. **C) 36 km/h** — Speed = 150 m ÷ 15 s = 10 m/s; converting, 10 × 18/5 = 36 km/h.
11. **B) 6 days** — Combined rate = 1/10 + 1/15 = 3/30 + 2/30 = 5/30 = 1/6, so together they take 6 days.
12. **C) ₹210** — CI = P(1+R/100)ᵀ − P = 1000×(1.1)² − 1000 = 1210 − 1000 = 210.
13. **C) 32 L** — Each removal-and-replace step multiplies remaining milk by (1 − 10/50) = 0.8. After 2 steps: 50 × 0.8² = 50 × 0.64 = 32 L.
14. **B) 17 cm** — 8, 15, 17 is a standard Pythagorean triple: 8² + 15² = 64 + 225 = 289 = 17².
15. **B) 5/14** — P(both red) = C(5,2)/C(8,2) = 10/28 = 5/14.
16. **C) 32** — The series is powers of 2 (2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64); each term doubles the previous one.
17. **B) 38** — The differences between consecutive terms are the odd numbers 3, 5, 7, 9, 11: 3+3=6, 6+5=11, 11+7=18, 18+9=27, 27+11=38.
18. **A) EPH** — Shift every letter forward by 1: D→E, O→P, G→H, giving EPH.
19. **A) WLT** — Mirror mapping (letter position p ↔ 27−p): D→W, O→L, G→T, giving WLT.
20. **C) Potato** — Apple, Banana, and Mango are fruits; Potato is a vegetable.
21. **B) School** — A doctor's workplace is a hospital; a teacher's workplace (the institution, not a room inside it) is a school.
22. **A) Sister** — "My grandfather's only son" (since the man himself isn't that son) must be the man's father. The woman is that son's (the father's) daughter, so she is the man's sister.
23. **C) 5 km** — The two legs (3 km and 4 km) are at right angles, so the straight-line distance is √(3²+4²) = √25 = 5 km (the 3-4-5 triple).
24. **B) J** — The series skips one letter each time (B, D, F, H, J), i.e., every second letter of the alphabet.
25. **C) 20** — 9, 16, and 25 are all perfect squares (3², 4², 5²); 20 is not a perfect square.
26. **B) Receive** — Correct spelling follows "i before e except after c": rec-e-i-ve.
27. **B) goes** — Third-person singular subject "She" takes the verb form "goes" in the present simple tense.
28. **A) Modern** — "Ancient" means very old; its opposite is "Modern." ("Old," "Historic," and "Aged" are synonyms or near-synonyms of "Ancient," not antonyms.)
29. **C) He doesn't like coffee.** — Third-person singular subject "He" needs the auxiliary "doesn't," followed by the base form of the verb "like."
30. **B) Joyful** — "Happy" and "Joyful" both describe a state of pleasure or contentment; the other options are near-opposites or unrelated in tone.
