# Phase 01: Monolithic Architecture

Before diving into Microservices and Cloud Native architectures, it is critical to understand the traditional approach: the Monolith. Microservices were designed to solve specific problems that arise when monoliths scale. Without understanding those problems, adopting microservices often leads to disastrous complexity (the "Distributed Monolith").

## Learning Objectives

In this phase, you will learn:
- What defines a monolithic architecture.
- The advantages and disadvantages of monoliths.
- How monolithic applications scale (Vertical vs. Horizontal).
- The tipping points: when and why an organization should consider migrating from a monolith to microservices.

## Files in this Phase

1. `01-What-is-a-Monolith.md`: Core characteristics, benefits, and drawbacks.
2. `02-Scaling-Monoliths.md`: Vertical scaling (Scale Up) vs Horizontal scaling (Scale Out) and the limitations of shared state.
3. `03-When-to-Migrate.md`: Identifying the technical and organizational signals that indicate a monolith has outgrown its usefulness.
